Why is NVIDIA hand-delivering CPUs to OpenAI, Anthropic, and AWS?

For the past 3 years, the tech industry focused almost exclusively on GPUs. But as AI models evolved from simple chatbots into autonomous agents, a major bottleneck emerged: tool calling, code execution, and sandbox orchestration don't run on GPUs—they run on CPUs.

To solve this, NVIDIA has officially started shipping 'Vera'—its first custom CPU purpose-built for the age of agentic AI. Packing 88 custom Olympus cores and 1.2TB/s memory bandwidth, Vera is engineered to feed GPUs and handle continuous agent workflows up to 1.8x faster per core.

OCI plans to deploy hundreds of thousands of Vera CPUs, while AWS, OpenAI, Anthropic, and SpaceXAI are integrating it into their next-gen stacks.
