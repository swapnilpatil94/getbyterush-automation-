Why is Amazon putting NVIDIA tech inside its custom AI chips? 🧩

For years, big cloud providers built proprietary silicon (like AWS Trainium) to escape NVIDIA’s high GPU costs.

But as AI models scale past trillions of parameters, physical hardware constraints hit a wall: standard memory controllers consume up to 25% of the primary compute die space.

NVIDIA's answer is NVHBM—moving the memory controller off the compute die and directly into the 3D HBM base stack.

The results:
⚡ +30% Memory Bandwidth vs HBM4E
🔋 15% Lower HBM Power Consumption
📐 25% More Compute Space freed on the XPU

Recognizing the leap, Amazon’s Annapurna Labs announced it will integrate NVHBM and NVLink Fusion starting with Trainium4—allowing AWS custom chips and NVIDIA GPUs to co-exist in shared rack architectures.

Even in the intense custom chip war, physics and efficiency win out.
