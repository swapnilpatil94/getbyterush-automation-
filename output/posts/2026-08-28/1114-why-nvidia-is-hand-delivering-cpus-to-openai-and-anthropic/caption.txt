Why is NVIDIA hand-delivering CPUs to OpenAI, Anthropic, and AWS?

For the past three years, the AI narrative revolved around one thing: GPUs. But as models evolve from answering simple questions to acting autonomously—running code, managing sandboxes, and executing multi-step workflows—a new bottleneck has emerged: the CPU.

NVIDIA's VP of Hyperscale Ian Buck recently hand-delivered the first production units of Vera—NVIDIA’s first custom CPU purpose-built for agentic AI.

Here’s what’s changing:
• Every Python code generation, tool call, and agent orchestration runs on the CPU, not the GPU.
• Vera packs 88 custom Olympus cores with 1.2TB/s memory bandwidth to sustain continuous agent workloads.
• Oracle Cloud Infrastructure is deploying hundreds of thousands of Vera CPUs, alongside adoption by AWS, Anthropic, SpaceXAI, and OpenAI.

AI compute is no longer just about raw matrix math—it's about how fast your system can orchestrate autonomous work.
