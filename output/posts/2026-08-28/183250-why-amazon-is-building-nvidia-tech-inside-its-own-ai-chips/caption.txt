Why Amazon is building NVIDIA tech inside its own AI chips

Amazon and NVIDIA are fierce rivals in AI silicon, yet Amazon's chip arm (Annapurna Labs) just adopted NVIDIA's new NVHBM memory and NVLink interconnect standard for Trainium4. This story exposes a fundamental architecture shift in AI hardware: memory controllers are moving off the main chip to free up 25% more compute die space.
