NVIDIA just took aim at the biggest barrier to enterprise AI agents: operating costs. Instead of sending every request to massive frontier models, NVIDIA's new Nemotron 3.5 Lightning (30B MoE) and NeMo Switchyard open-source router dynamically assign tasks. In benchmarks with LangChain, intelligent routing achieved 74% lower costs by sending only 7% of calls to expensive frontier models—while preserving accuracy.
