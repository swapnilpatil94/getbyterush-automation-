How many AI assistants does a company actually need? HR platform HiBob didn't settle for just one—they deployed 2,500 custom GPTs using ChatGPT Enterprise across their product and teams. By tailoring AI tools to micro-workflows rather than forcing staff into a generic chatbot, HiBob boosted internal AI adoption and used those insights to drive new revenue features in the Bob platform.
