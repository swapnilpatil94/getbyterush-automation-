Power is officially the defining constraint on AI expansion. Today, NVIDIA, Google, and Emerald AI launched the AI Energy Management Alliance (AEMA) to build data centers that dynamically manage electricity based on grid demand. Instead of acting as static power sinks, these 'flexible data centers' can shift compute workloads, trigger stored battery power, or curtail usage during peak grid stress. In exchange, utilities can grant faster grid connections without waiting for expensive infrastructure upgrades.
