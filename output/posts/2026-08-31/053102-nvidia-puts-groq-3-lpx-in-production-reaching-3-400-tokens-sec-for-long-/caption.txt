NVIDIA just put its Groq 3 LPX inference platform into full production, achieving 3,400 tokens per second on 100,000-token long-context workloads in Gemma 4 31B benchmarks. As AI shifts from single chat prompts to continuous, multi-agent reasoning, decode latency has become the primary bottleneck. By codesigning Rubin GPUs with up to 256 Groq 3 LP30 LPUs per rack, NVIDIA is aiming to resolve the tradeoff between throughput and response speed. Key details from the release: • 4x faster token generation on Gemma 4 31B compared to nearest platforms. • SpaceXAI adopting Vera CPUs for agentic orchestration from Earth data centers to orbital satellites. • Cloud providers like Nebius and CoreWeave deploying LPX and Spectrum-X Multiplane to build dedicated 'Token Factories'.
