Why do AI models get worse right before they get dramatically better? 🧠 Classical statistical theory dictates that adding model capacity eventually leads to overfitting. But in deep neural networks, performance follows a phenomenon known as 'Deep Double Descent.' As model size or training time increases, test error initially drops, then spikes right around the interpolation threshold, before dropping a second time into state-of-the-art performance. Here is how scaling breaks standard intuition — and why stopping training early can be a costly mistake.
