Microsoft and NVIDIA just announced a massive architecture shift for Windows PCs: persistent local AI agents. Instead of sending prompts to cloud servers, Windows is building native OS primitives called Microsoft Execution Containers (MXC) to govern AI agents executing safely in the background. To power these background agents, NVIDIA introduced RTX Spark — offering up to 128GB of unified memory on laptops like the Surface Laptop Ultra to run 125B parameter models unmetered and offline.
