Why do language models hallucinate? It’s easy to assume AI hallucinations are random glitches, but new research from OpenAI highlights a deeper cause: how models are evaluated and trained. When evaluation frameworks prioritize continuous output and reward plausible responses, models are disincentivized from admitting uncertainty. The fix? Advanced evaluation systems designed to measure factuality, reward honesty, and detect hallucination patterns before deployment.
