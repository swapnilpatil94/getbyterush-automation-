NVIDIA just made a major change to how AI hardware is built. As AI models reach trillion-parameter scales, standard HBM architecture runs into a physical bottleneck: memory controllers on the main XPU die take up precious silicon area. NVIDIA's new NVHBM technology moves the memory controller directly into the 3D HBM stack. The technical gains according to NVIDIA: • Up to 30% greater memory bandwidth • Up to 15% lower memory power consumption • Frees up to 25% compute area on the main die In a surprising move, Amazon’s Annapurna Labs is the first official partner, planning to support NVLink Fusion with its next-generation Trainium4 chips.
