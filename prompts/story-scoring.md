## FRESHNESS

10 = happened within hours

8 = within 24 hours

5 = several days old but developing

2 = old/recycled

0 = stale

---

## IMPORTANCE

10 = potentially major technology/business/internet shift

7 = meaningful development for a large audience

5 = useful but limited impact

2 = minor

0 = irrelevant

---

## CURIOSITY

10 = creates a powerful unanswered question

7 = clearly interesting

5 = moderately interesting

2 = predictable

0 = no curiosity

---

## SURPRISE

10 = genuinely unexpected

7 = counterintuitive

5 = somewhat surprising

2 = obvious

0 = expected

---

## STORY TENSION

10 = strong setup → conflict → reveal

7 = clear narrative

5 = some narrative potential

2 = mostly informational

0 = no story

0 = unlikely to be shared

7 = useful later

# DECISION

90–100:
BREAKING / PRIORITY

85–89:
PUBLISH NOW

75–84:
STRONG STORY / QUEUE

65–74:
WATCHLIST

Below 65:
REJECT

---

# IMPORTANT

A high score cannot compensate for lack of evidence.

If the core claim cannot be verified:

DO NOT PUBLISH.

If the story is old:

DO NOT manufacture freshness.

If the story is interesting but speculative:

LABEL IT AS SPECULATION.

Never convert uncertainty into certainty for engagement.
